    How the numbers are calculated

    Each engine score is turned into a winning chance. A move's cost is how much winning chance it gives away compared with the engine's best move. Up to 2% lost is Excellent, up to 5% Good, up to 10% an Inaccuracy, up to 20% a Mistake, more is a Blunder. A move that matches the engine's first choice is Best.

    A Miss is a move that costs more than 10% right after the opponent's own mistake, miss or blunder, while the position stays playable. In other words, a chance to punish an error that was not taken.

    Accuracy is a 0 to 100 score built from those costs, with sharp positions weighted more. It follows the published Lichess formula, so expect a few points of difference from chess.com, which keeps its exact formula private. Average loss is the mean centipawn loss per move.
